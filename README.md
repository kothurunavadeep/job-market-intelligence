# 💼 Job Market Intelligence & Skills Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes job postings to identify in-demand technical
skills, salary patterns, popular job roles, and hiring trends.

It aims to help job seekers understand market demand and help
organizations understand the skills employers are looking for.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Identify the most in-demand technical skills.
- Compare demand for Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and other skills.
- Analyze salary ranges across job roles.
- Compare job opportunities by location and experience level.
- Identify the skills most frequently requested by employers.
- Explore changes in hiring demand across available time periods.
